Inventory mod for Left 4 Dead 2 (game version 2.2.4.3)
=====================================================

What is in this folder
  inventory_mod.dll           the mod
  injector.exe                loads the mod into the running game
  load-mod.bat                runs the injector for you
  inventory_mod.example.toml  rules (box count, weight limit, item weights), for whoever hosts
  inventory_mod_screamer.jpg  the infection's screamer picture
  addons\                     the mod's addons, which the mod loads from there (keep the folder next to the DLL):
    inventory_mod_rounds.vpk  the ammo pack of each round type


1. Start the game in insecure mode (important)
   Steam > Library > right click Left 4 Dead 2 > Properties > General > Launch options:
       -insecure
   The mod is a DLL loaded into the game. Never use it on VAC-secured servers: that can get your account banned.
   With -insecure the game only lists / joins insecure servers, so that cannot happen by accident.

2. Start Left 4 Dead 2 and wait for the main menu.

3. Double-click load-mod.bat
   It prints "loaded ... into left4dead2.exe". The mod writes inventory_mod.log next to the DLL.
   Windows Defender or another antivirus may complain about injector.exe (loading a DLL into another program is
   what cheats do too). Allow it, or run it once from "More info > Run anyway".
   Do it again every time you start the game (the mod is not installed into the game, nothing is changed on disk).
   With addons\inventory_mod_rounds.vpk, dropped rounds look like an ammo pack of their type (rifle, SMG,
   shotgun, ...). Everyone in the game needs the mod with that file: whoever lacks it sees a red ERROR model.

4. Join the game
   The inventory lives on the server, so the one who hosts must run the mod as well:
     - a game hosted by a friend who runs the mod (listen server): just join it;
     - a dedicated server (srcds.exe) that runs the mod: join it (it must be started with -insecure too).
   On a server without the mod, the panel stays empty.
   Playing alone or hosting yourself works too: then your own game keeps the inventory.

Keys (rebind them in the window's Settings tab)
  I                 open / close the inventory window (Settings, Debug, drag and drop, right click menu)
  middle mouse      hold: the radial wheel. Point at something and let go to take it in hand,
                    right click drops it. You can keep walking while it is open.
  mouse wheel, [ ]  step through gun, melee, stored weapons, then the items
  3 .. 9, 0         take inventory box 1 .. 8 in hand
  ;                 store what is in your hands (items and weapons)
  H                 pick up the item / weapon in front of you into the inventory
  G                 drop what is in your hands
  E                 (the game's use) on an item or weapon whose game slot you already fill: it goes into the inventory
  Ctrl+End          unload the mod

Slots, wheel or both
  Settings tab > Inventory view: "Slots and wheel" (default), "Slots only" (the middle mouse button stays the game's)
  or "Wheel only" (no item boxes under the game's gun and melee at all, the wheel shows everything; the number keys
  and the mouse wheel work the game's way again; hold the middle mouse button for the wheel).

The weight
  Everything you carry counts (stored items, the items in your slots, your gun and melee, your reserve rounds). The
  bar under the boxes turns red when nothing more fits. The host's inventory_mod.toml decides the limit and what things
  weigh; copy inventory_mod.example.toml to inventory_mod.toml next to the DLL and edit it if you host.

Rounds
  The window's Items tab (I) lists the rounds you carry and what they weigh. "Drop 50" (a magazine's worth) or
  "Drop all" puts them on the ground as an ammo pack, tinted the colour of the round type, with exactly those rounds;
  anyone takes them back with E. With addons\inventory_mod_rounds.vpk each round type is its own ammo pack. The wheel (middle mouse) shows them too, in their colours: right click there drops
  a magazine's worth, holding the right button half a second drops all of that type. An ammo pile only fills you up to the weight limit.
  In the wheel, each segment is as large as its share of what you carry weighs.
  With [ammo_piles] in the host's inventory_mod.toml, a map's ammo pile holds 1 to 3 magazines of a random round
  type, shown as that type's ammo pack, and a few more packs lie near the map's items. Take what you can carry; the
  rest stays, and an empty pack is gone.
  With [map_guns], a gun found in the map has only part of its magazine (10 to 60 %) the first time it is picked up,
  and a weapon spot of the map gives one weapon, then it is gone.

The infection (when the host's inventory_mod.toml has [infection])
  Knocked down by the infected, grabbed by a special or vomited on by a boomer, you are infected. There is no clock on
  the screen: your sight gets sicker (sway, blur, veins, a racing heartbeat) as time runs out. Every new hit takes time
  off. Pain pills do not cure it, they put the clock back to the start. If it runs out you die, a special infected rises where you stood, and you only come back next chapter
  (no defibrillator, no rescue closet).
  You cough now and then (everyone hears it), more often as the clock runs out. Near the end you go insane: you
  laugh for no reason or scream at things that are not there.
  The sound gets muffled and warped with a heartbeat under it, and when the clock runs out a screamer jumps at you.
  Turn any of it off in the window's Settings tab ("Infection"): screen effect, sound, screamer. "Screamer chance"
  makes the screamer random: 0 never, 1 always, 0.5 half the time.
  inventory_mod_screamer.jpg (next to the DLL) is the screamer's picture; put another picture there to change it.

Stamina (when the host's inventory_mod.toml has [stamina])
  Running, jumping, melee swings and shoves use stamina; it comes back when you rest a moment. Run out and you are
  exhausted until it is back to about a third: you move slower, swing and shove slower, and your melee hits do less
  damage. The game's own shove tiredness is off meanwhile: stamina is the limit.
  A thin bar under the crosshair shows it while it is not full, and the screen's edges darken as it runs low. Turn
  either off in the window's Settings tab ("Stamina").

Files the mod writes next to the DLL
  inventory_mod_settings.toml  your settings and keys (the Settings tab writes it)
  inventory_mod.inventory  your inventory when you play alone / host (on someone else's server it is kept there)
  inventory_mod.log        what it did, for when something goes wrong
